Unofficial fix for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2944488802]Melee Animation[/url].

[h2]Fixes this error repeating every frame[/h2][code][MeleeAnim] Rendering exception when doing animation ...
System.NullReferenceException
  at Verse.PawnRenderNodeWorker_Body_Tattoo.CanDrawNow
  at Verse.PawnRenderTree.ParallelPreDraw
  at AM.Patches.Patch_PawnRenderer_RenderPawnAt.Prefix[/code]
Also fixes random pawns flashing bright for a single frame and losing their shadow while any melee animation is playing (even when paused).

Symptoms of the error: the game becomes choppy (pawns appear to move at low FPS). It happens most often when you open a map where fighting started while you were looking elsewhere (e.g. Dynamic Diplomacy NPC battles).

[b]Cause:[/b]
1) Melee Animation's RenderPawnAt prefix uses the renderer's cached pre-render results without checking that they were filled. If the pawn was not pre-rendered this frame, the draw parameters are empty and the render tree throws.
2) An animation only honours its pending destroy after drawing its pawns, so once the draw throws, a finished animation is never removed and the error repeats forever.
3) Melee Animation looks up "is this pawn animated?" through a one-entry cache shared by all threads, and reads it outside its lock. RimWorld 1.6 pre-renders pawns on several threads at once, so a pawn can receive another pawn's animation. Melee Animation then reports it as invisible, and for that frame it is drawn with the invisibility shader and no shadow.

[b]Fix:[/b]
1) Right before Melee Animation draws an animated pawn, fill the missing results the same way vanilla RenderPawnAt does. Pawns not being animated are not touched.
2) If an animation's draw throws after it has finished, it is destroyed anyway.
3) The lookup goes straight to Melee Animation's own pawn-to-animation table instead of the shared cache.

[h2]Performance: render tree rebuilt on every draw[/h2]While a pawn is being animated (duels, executions, grapples), Melee Animation's [i]RenderPawnAt[/i] prefix calls [i]PawnRenderTree.SetDirty()[/i] on every draw, several times per frame. This throws away the pawn's whole render tree and re-resolves every body, head and apparel graphic. With Humanoid Alien Races, a single duel measured about 8 ms per frame in Dubs Performance Analyzer, with spikes over 100 ms during bigger fights.

[b]Fix:[/b] The rebuild is now skipped except for severed heads, which genuinely need it. Vanilla's render tree already refreshes its draw requests whenever the pose, facing or render flags change, so animations still look the same. In a full-scale battle the same measurement dropped from about 8 ms per frame to under 0.1 ms.

[h2]Performance: lasso range ring[/h2]Hovering an execution or lasso option in the right-click menu (for unavailable ones, the "?" cooldown icon) shows the lasso range ring. Melee Animation recalculated that ring every frame, with a line-of-sight check for every cell in range (up to about 1,800 cells with the best lasso).

[b]Fix:[/b] The visible cells are cached and drawn around the pawn's current position. Line of sight is recalculated at most 4 times a second while the pawn walks and once a second while it stands still (so opened doors and new walls still show). The ring looks the same.

Note: most of the stutter while any right-click menu is open comes from vanilla regenerating the whole menu every 4th frame, not from Melee Animation.

[h2]Automatic lassos ignored invisibility[/h2]Pawns with a lasso automatically lassoed (and lasso-executed) enemies they should not be able to perceive: psychically invisible pawns and hidden Anomaly entities such as revenants. Vanilla targeting ignores both. Sleeping targets (dormant mechanoids, sleeping pawns) were lassoed too, then quietly walked back to where they were.

[b]Fix:[/b] Invisible and sleeping pawns are removed from the automatic lasso and execution target list. Once revealed or awake, they can be targeted again. Manual orders from the right-click menu are unchanged.

[h2]Lassos ignored the cover setting[/h2]Melee Animation's "Max Building Fill For Lasso Drag" setting is meant to stop lassos from pulling pawns over partial cover such as sandbags. It had no effect on the lasso itself: RimWorld's line-of-sight check drops Melee Animation's extra rule for single-cell targets (nearly every pawn), so only the range ring used it. The rule also let through every cell a pawn can walk on, so even the ring never treated sandbags or barricades as cover.

[b]Fix:[/b] The lasso check now uses the rule too, so the range ring and the actual lasso agree. Any building with a fill above the setting blocks the lasso; open doors still let it through. Furniture the target is standing or sitting on does not count. At the default 20%, chairs, beds and tables (35-40%) block it too. To let lassos pass furniture but not sandbags or barricades (55%), set it to 50%.

[h2]Automatic duels interrupted rituals[/h2]An automatic friendly duel picks its partner without checking whether that pawn is busy, and starts the duel on them directly. A psychic ritual invoker pulled into a duel dropped the offering, and the ritual failed for a missing offering.

[b]Fix:[/b] Pawns that are part of a ritual, gathering or other group activity are no longer chosen for automatic friendly duels. Duels you start yourself are unchanged.

[h2]RimWorld 1.7: duel bow faces north, weapon disappears[/h2]On 1.7, pawns bowed facing north before a friendly duel, and one duelist's weapon vanished during the duel. Melee Animation stores fixed facing and visibility as stepped curve keys, which 1.7's newer Unity evaluates wrongly.

[b]Fix:[/b] Those keys are converted to flat ones with the same value when an animation loads. No change on 1.6.

[h2]RimWorld 1.7: weapon trails look short[/h2]Since RimWorld 1.7.5011, weapon trails were drawn only in short fragments. Melee Animation builds trails from four-sided shapes, and the new 1.7 engine reads them as triangles, so most of the trail ends up misaligned.

[b]Fix:[/b] On 1.7, each shape is split into two triangles before the trail is drawn. No change on 1.6.

[h2]Right-click menu options[/h2]New toggles in Melee Animation's own settings ([i]Other[/i] tab, off by default) hide its right-click entries: all of them while the pawn is undrafted, only the greyed-out ones, or lasso / execution / skill entries separately. Automatic lassos and executions are unchanged.

[h2]Minor log cleanups[/h2][list]
[*]"AM.Events.XxxEvent must be instantiated using the ScriptableObject.CreateInstance method": animation events are now created with [i]ScriptableObject.CreateInstance[/i].
[*]"[MeleeAnim] Started twice!" when loading a save with an animation in progress: the redundant start call is skipped quietly.
[*]"Mesh.vertices is too small" / "Mesh.colors is out of bounds" during duels: the weapon trail mesh is cleared before it is rebuilt with fewer points.
[*]"Failed to find random verb" during executions by a pawn holding a ranged weapon with a melee sidearm (Simple Sidearms): the damage now uses the equipped weapon or bare hands instead of failing.
[*]"[MeleeAnim] ... has bad job ... Cancelling animation" and "[MeleeAnim] Accidentally killed or downed ..." during fights are logged as warnings, so they no longer open the log window in dev mode. Both are routine outcomes that Melee Animation already handles; its other errors are unchanged.
[/list]
